Frequently Asked Questions

Is the AI Vaidya a replacement for a real doctor?
No. Absolutely not. The AI Vaidya is an informational tool designed to provide general guidance on Ayurvedic principles, lifestyle, and remedies for common, non-serious concerns. It is not a medical professional and cannot diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or replace a consultation with a qualified healthcare provider.
How is my personal and health data protected?
We take your privacy very seriously. All personal data is stored on secure, encrypted Google Cloud servers, compliant with Indian data laws. We use blockchain-inspired principles for data integrity and employ active security monitoring. We will never sell your personal health information. Please see our full Privacy Policy for details.
How does the "Freemium" model work?
Our freemium model gives you free access to all our AI-powered tools, including the AI Vaidya chat, the Herb Scanner, and our knowledge base. This allows you to explore and learn about Ayurveda at no cost. For services that require a certified professional, such as one-on-one video consultations for diagnosis and treatment, we charge a fee.
Are the practitioners on the telemedicine platform certified?
Yes. We have a strict verification process. All Ayurvedic practitioners available for telemedicine consultations are required to provide valid certifications (such as a B.A.M.S. degree) and proof of registration with the relevant government bodies, like the Ministry of AYUSH.
How accurate is the Ayurvedic Herb Scanner?
Our Herb Scanner uses advanced AI vision models (Gemini) trained on a vast database of plants. While it is highly accurate, its identification can be affected by image quality, lighting, and the angle. The information provided is for educational purposes. You must consult a qualified expert before consuming any plant or herb.
